Loading the floor

Jury is a judging floor that lives in your team chat. Every submission gets its own room on Switch. Three AI judges gather evidence and argue it out. A human gives every verdict.

03 / Leaderboard

Every case, ranked

Ranked by verdict first (shortlist, maybe, undecided, pass), then by score. Stars are the score out of 100 on a five-star scale. Click a row for the full case file and a ready-to-paste verdict.

04 / The bench

Who judges what

Three AI judges, each owning part of the rubric, and a human who owns the verdict. The Auditor is plain code, so it cannot be argued with. The others are agents on Switch and can be swapped for any agent you like.
